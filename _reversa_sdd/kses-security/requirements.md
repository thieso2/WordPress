## Overview

KSES Strips Evil Scripts: an HTML allowlist filter and the last line of defense for untrusted markup. 🟢

**Complexity:** `high` · **Business rules extracted:** 10 · **Public functions catalogued:** 10 · **Extension points:** 4

**Depended on by 4 module(s):** `comments`, `feeds`, `formatting-and-sanitization`, `http-api`

## Responsibilities

- **four-step scheme normalization** — Decode entities, strip whitespace, remove null bytes, lowercase; each step answers a specific historical XSS bypass technique before the allowlist comparison 🟢
- **multi-encoding colon detection** — preg_split on ':' plus its numeric, hex and named entity encodings, with a prior repair pass for truncated colon entities 🟢
- **depth-capped nested scheme recursion** — feed: is allowed, so what follows it is re-examined recursively with a hard cap of 2 levels, defeating feed:javascript: while keeping feed: support 🟢
