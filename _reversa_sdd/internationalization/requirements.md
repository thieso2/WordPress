## Overview

Translate strings, resolve the active locale, and switch locale mid-request. 🟢

**Complexity:** `medium` · **Business rules extracted:** 9 · **Public functions catalogued:** 10 · **Extension points:** 11

**Depended on by 2 module(s):** `formatting-and-sanitization`, `script-modules-and-assets`

## Responsibilities

- **locale precedence chain** — pre_determine_locale filter, then login-screen wp_lang (the only user-supplied source, sanitized), then user locale for admin and ?_locale=user JSON, then installation language, then the site option 🟢
- **stack-based locale switching** — switch_to_locale pushes and reloads all textdomains; restore_previous_locale pops and reloads again 🟢
