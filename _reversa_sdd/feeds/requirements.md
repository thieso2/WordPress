## Overview

Publish content as RSS 2.0, RSS 0.92, RDF and Atom, and consume external feeds via SimplePie. 🟢

**Complexity:** `medium` · **Business rules extracted:** 6 · **Public functions catalogued:** 3 · **Extension points:** 5

## Responsibilities

- **vendored-library integration by substitution** — SimplePie's HTTP transport is replaced by WP_SimplePie_File (routing through the WP HTTP API) and its sanitizer by WP_SimplePie_Sanitize_KSES, so the library's two riskiest behaviors use core's audited implementations 🟢
