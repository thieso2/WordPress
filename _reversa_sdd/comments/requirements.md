## Overview

Accept, moderate and display reader responses plus trackbacks and pingbacks. The moderation pipeline is the most rule-dense business logic in WordPress core: a spam filter expressed as option-driven sequential checks. 🟢

**Complexity:** `high` · **Business rules extracted:** 14 · **Public functions catalogued:** 15 · **Extension points:** 20

**Depended on by 3 module(s):** `blocks-library`, `rest-api`, `xmlrpc`

## Responsibilities

- **duplicate detection** — Concatenated prepare() fragments matching post, parent, author, optional email and exact content, excluding trashed comments. A hit terminates with HTTP 409 🟢
- **flood window query with defaulting-false verdict** — One-hour lookback keyed on user_id or IP, OR email; the verdict is delegated to comment_flood_filter which core never sets true 🟢
- **four-rule moderation** — Manual moderation short-circuit, link-count threshold, keyword substring match across six fields, previously-approved requirement 🟢
- **post-decision disallowed override** — The disallowed list runs after the approval decision and overrides it, choosing trash or spam based on EMPTY_TRASH_DAYS 🟢
