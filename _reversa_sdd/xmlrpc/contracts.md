## Risks

- 🟢 **F-XR-01** — A 211 KB always-on endpoint accepting credentials on every call is the largest legacy attack surface in WordPress; four of its six method families serve clients that no longer exist.
- 🟢 **F-XR-02** — Per-call credentials with no rate limiting make xmlrpc.php the canonical brute-force target; core supplies no throttle, exactly as with comment flooding.
- 🟢 **F-XR-03** — pingback.ping is a server-side fetch of an attacker-supplied URL, structurally identical to the oEmbed discovery risk but reachable unauthenticated.
- 🟢 **F-XR-04** — REST duplicates nearly all of wp.*; two complete remote APIs are maintained in parallel, one with schemas and permission callbacks and one without.
