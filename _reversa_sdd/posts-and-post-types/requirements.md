## Overview

The central content entity. wp_posts stores posts, pages, attachments, revisions, nav menu items, reusable blocks, templates, global styles and fonts: 16 built-in types in one table distinguished only by the post_type column. 🟢

**Complexity:** `high` · **Business rules extracted:** 12 · **Public functions catalogued:** 14 · **Extension points:** 19

**Depended on by 17 module(s):** `block-editor`, `blocks-library`, `comments`, `customizer`, `embeds-oembed`, `global-styles-theme-json`, `media-and-attachments`, `multisite`, `query-and-loop`, `rest-api`, `rewrite-and-permalinks`, `sitemaps`, `taxonomy-and-terms`, `themes-and-templates`, `users-roles-capabilities`, `widgets-and-nav-menus`, `xmlrpc`

## Responsibilities

- **status derived from date** — publish with a date >= 60s ahead becomes future; future with a date < 60s away becomes publish. Status is not caller-controlled 🟢
- **three-scope slug uniqueness** — attachments unique across all types; hierarchical types unique within (type, parent) including attachments; flat types unique within type. Reserved names (feeds, 'embed', pagination numbers, date-archive segments) force a suffix. do-while loop with one query per iteration 🟢
- **dynamic transition hooks** — do_action("{old_status}_to_{new_status}") generates up to 121 undeclared hook names from the 11 built-in statuses 🟢
- **transition side effects** — GUID seeding on first publish, lastpostmodified/lastpostdate cache invalidation across three timezones, post-count cache invalidation, and unconditional publish_future_post cron clearing 🟢
